Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Digital Larder is a personal grocery and recipe app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Who We Are

Digital Larder is an independent app developed by Christoph Heitmann. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: christoph.heitmann96@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the app:

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account via Supabase
  • Chef AI messages — the text you type in the Chef AI chat is sent to our server to generate recipe suggestions

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, payment information, or any other personal data.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your iPhone and never sent to our servers:

  • Your grocery lists and categories
  • Your saved recipes
  • Your recipe ratings and custom names

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and remains on your device. Deleting the app removes all local data.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Email address — to authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Chef AI messages — forwarded to a large language model (LLM) to generate recipe suggestions. Messages are not stored after the response is returned.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your Chef AI messages are processed by a third-party LLM provider to generate responses. This is covered by their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Your account is protected by Supabase authentication infrastructure

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your account — contact us at the email below and we will remove all your data
  • Delete local data — simply delete the app from your iPhone
  • Request your data — contact us and we will provide any data we hold about you

8. Children's Privacy

Digital Larder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

The Bag

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

The Bag is an iOS app for shopping lists, recipes and an AI cooking assistant ("Chef AI"). This policy explains which personal data the app processes, why, who receives it, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

The Bag is developed and operated by crystovh (independent developer).
Contact for all privacy matters: betreffen_skalar.2n@icloud.com

2. What Data We Process, Why, and on Which Legal Basis

2.1 Account

To use the app you need an account. We process your email address, your password (stored only as a hash) and the display name you choose. The display name is shown to people you share a shopping list with.

Purpose: creating and securing your account, password reset, showing you to list members.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Providing an email address is required to create an account.

2.2 Shopping lists

Your shopping lists are stored on our servers so they can be synced and shared. This includes list names, categories, items, quantities, status and notes, the members and roles of each list, and invitations you create. Items record which member created or changed them.

If you share a list, other members of that list can see its content and your display name.

Purpose: providing the shopping list feature, syncing between devices and list members in real time.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.3 Chef AI

When you use Chef AI, the messages you type, any photos you attach, and links to recipe pages you send are transmitted to our server and forwarded to an AI language model to generate an answer. Our server also runs an automated safety check on each request (text and images) and blocks requests that violate our usage policy.

Depending on your question, the AI may perform a web search for recipes. The search terms are generated from your message and sent to a search provider (see section 3). Recipe URLs you send are fetched by our server.

Chat history: your text messages and the AI's replies are stored in your account so you can continue conversations later. Photos are not stored; they are only used to answer the message they were attached to. If a request is blocked by the safety check, we store the blocked message together with the category and reason for blocking.

Purpose: providing the Chef AI assistant and your conversation history; preventing misuse of the AI service.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the assistant and history; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing misuse) for the safety check.

2.4 Technical data

When the app communicates with our servers, technical data such as your IP address, request time, and app/device information sent by iOS is processed and may appear in short-lived server logs.

Purpose: delivering the service, security, troubleshooting.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functioning service).

2.5 Data that stays on your device

Your recipe library (saved recipes, favorites, ratings, planned meals, recipe categories), your app settings (e.g. language) and the shopping-mode Live Activity are stored only on your iPhone and are not sent to our servers. Deleting the app removes this data.

Camera access is only used when you choose to take a photo for Chef AI. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Retention

  • Account and shopping lists — until you delete them or your account.
  • Chat history — until you delete individual conversations or all conversations in the app, or your account.
  • Photos sent to Chef AI — not stored; processed only for that request.
  • Server logs — kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, chat history, list memberships and all shopping lists you own, including for members you shared them with. Items you added to other people's lists remain in those lists but are no longer linked to you. All app data on your device is removed as well. If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion by email at the address in section 1.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data erased (Art. 17),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, contact us at betreffen_skalar.2n@icloud.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

6. Automated Decisions

The safety check described in section 2.3 automatically blocks requests that violate our usage policy. It only affects whether Chef AI answers a message and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. We do not carry out profiling.

7. Security

  • All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security so that users can only access their own data and lists shared with them.

8. Children

The Bag is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly process data of children under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the current version.
